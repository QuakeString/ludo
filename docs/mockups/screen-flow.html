Seats & the dice

Every seat has its own dice place

The die is never parked in the middle. It travels to whoever is on turn and sits beside their own home, so you always roll from your own corner.

Pass & play — sit facing each other

2 playersopposite ends
4 playerstwo opposite pairs
Each player's name and die face their own edge of the phone, so nobody plays upside down.

Online — you are always bottom-left

Your seat is fixedthe board rotates, not you
Everyone sees themselves in the same place; opponents fill the remaining seats in turn order.
9 · Seats & dicethe die moves to the player on turn
Reading a six-seat board. With six colors in play, the seat list is grouped by team and your own team carries a blue rail down its left edge, so you can tell friend from foe without decoding six hues. On the board itself, the same job is done by the chip: your movable chips are the only ones that pulse.

Disconnects, now 5 minutes. Your seat is held for five minutes, not one — but the game does not sit frozen while everyone waits. AI plays your turns from the moment you drop, and the instant you return you take the seat back mid-game, with the moves made in your absence shown in the turn log.

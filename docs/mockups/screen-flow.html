7 · Game — 6 seats, teamshexagon, 3 chips each, team rail on the left
Reading a six-seat board. With six colors in play, the seat list is grouped by team and your own team carries a blue rail down its left edge, so you can tell friend from foe without decoding six hues. On the board itself, the same job is done by the chip: your movable chips are the only ones that pulse.

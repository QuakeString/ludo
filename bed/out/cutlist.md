## Timber

- Finished solid timber: **146.1 litres** (0.146 m3, about 110 kg in oak)
- Rough sawn to buy: **181.4 litres** (0.181 m3) before waste and defects -- add a further 20 % when ordering
- Plywood: 1.13 m2 of 12 mm for the drawer bottoms

Glued-up panels (headboard, foot end) should be made from boards no wider than about 150 mm, alternating the growth rings, and left to settle before final thicknessing.

## Notes

- **Headboard panel** -- glued up from narrower boards; leave 3 mm play in the grooves top and bottom and do not glue it in
- **Head post** -- mortise before shaping; keep the bolt counterbore on the outside face
- **Foot end panel** -- glued up from narrower boards; leave 3 mm play in the grooves top and bottom and do not glue it in
- **Foot post** -- mortise before shaping; keep the bolt counterbore on the outside face
- **Side rail** -- bed bolts pull the joint up; the tenon only locates it
- **Slat bearer** -- glued and screwed to the inside of the rail, flush with nothing -- work off the top edge
- **Centre beam** -- carries the middle of every slat; screw each slat to it
- **Drawer front** -- routed finger pull, radiused; screwed to the box from inside
- **Drawer bottom** -- slides into the grooves as the box goes together
